# Code of Conduct

AI Creations Now repository discussions must remain professional, relevant, and respectful.

- Discuss the product, documentation, compatibility, or reproducible behavior in good faith.
- Do not post harassment, threats, discriminatory content, spam, deceptive downloads, or impersonation.
- Do not publish passwords, access tokens, MFA information, customer data, private tenant information, license keys, or security-sensitive logs.
- Do not use repository discussions to distribute modified or unofficial AI Creations Now executables.
- Follow GitHub's Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policies.

AI Creations Now may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect users, customers, systems, or the integrity of official product distribution.

For private matters, use https://aicreatenow.com/ or info@aicreatenow.com.
